The concept

Dinner, drinks, and the dirty work of AI implementation

Level Agency and growth advisor Akash Pathak are co-hosting an exclusive dinner event for senior marketing and growth leaders in Chicago on September 22.

Lisa Hurst opens with what AI is producing inside Level's client accounts right now: where it changed media efficiency, where it took time out of production, and the places we tried something that did not pay off. Akash takes the second half on the harder problem, which is implementation. How this work gets sequenced, staffed, and adopted across a large organization rather than stopping at the pilot.

The program is deliberately short because the room is the point. The rest of the night is good food and better conversation.

The conversation

What's on the table

01

Media efficiency, with the numbers

Where AI bidding and audience signal moved cost per outcome in Level's client accounts, what the tests looked like, and the cases where the lift did not hold up.

02

Time out of production

Creative and content workflows where AI compressed the calendar, plus a straight read on what it cost in review cycles and quality control.

03

Showing up in AI search

Generative engine optimization is live work today. What Level is running for clients now, and what a growth team can start on this quarter without waiting on a platform roadmap.

04

Getting past the pilot

Akash on sequencing, ownership, and staffing: the difference between an AI proof of concept and something that runs in production every day.

05

A high learn rate

Around 95 percent of Edison's ideas never succeeded. If 5 to 10 percent of your AI bets scale, you have a working pipeline. Building a portfolio that survives the misses is the skill.

06

Where human judgment stays

The calls that should not be handed to a model, and how teams keep taste and craft in the loop as they scale the work that should be automated.
